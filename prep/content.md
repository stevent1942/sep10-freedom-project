## Overview/Introduction/Context
* Touring allows people to see and view wonders of the world. INstead of staying in one place their entire life, they can explore and tour across the world seeing many different and interesting things.
* Touring is traveling around the world to see and discover new things that can't or wouldn't be possible in the area you live.
* Technology made touring even easier and faster to tour places you would want to go to. Apps and gadgets help people as a slice of life improvement for touring, providing support with translations, movement and capturing memories (pictures).

* Easily Accessible Things
 * Google maps, free to use and download. Only drawback is you require a mobile device.
 * Google translate, free to use and download. Only drawback is you require a mobile device.
 * Youtube, can be used before going on a trip at your home to have a quick sneak peek and view of what or where you're touring is going to be like. Can also be used with a mobile device for quick tutorials.

* Moderately Accessibility (Low-ish Cost and widely owned)
 *  Phone, they have many and multiple uses, such as using apps or the camera. They can be used in most situations and give you access to a lot of stuff like youtube and google. They are globally popular and cheap depending on the model.
 *  Battery, they are relatively cheap depending on the size and can be used to power and charge many things, including your phone.

* Lower Accessibility (Higher cost and Harder to get)
  * Cameras, even the cheapest ones can cost a fortune, some may even cost half the amount of a car. But the pictures, moments, memories they capture are higher quality than what you can get with a regular phone camera.
  * Plane tickets, their costs vary on where you want to go to, so they can be extremely expensive. They are also a one time use making it hard for you to continue traveling, as you need 1 to and back from where you're going.

## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* Google Translate
  * If you're going to a different country or continent, you'll need something to translate their native language, and that's where Google Translate comes in.
  * Google Translate is free, allowing anyone to have quick access without being locked behind a pay wall.
  * Has multiple languages to translate to or from and also has detect language.
  * [Logo](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/images/Translate_logo.max-1440x810.png)
* Google Maps
  * Google Maps can help you visualize and plan where you want to tour.
  * Allows you to see where countries and cities are as well as how long it will take to reach there.
  * Find attractions as well as hotels to places you want to tour.
  * [Logo](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a3/Google_Maps_icon_%282026%29.svg/1280px-Google_Maps_icon_%282026%29.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=thumbnail)
* Youtube
  * Allows you to watch videos on places you plan to go to, as it may fuel your interest or make you lose interest.
  * Find and discover religions as well as tradition and culture through other peoples blogs.
  * Watch videos on destination guides.
  * [Logo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ef/Youtube_logo.png?utm_source=commons.wikimedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original)

##### Hardware
* Camera
  * A camera can be used during a tour, as you can capture memories and take photos with it. Which that is one of the main factors of touring, memory.
  * A camera is portable and doesn't use as much battery as a phone as well as having clearer pictures.
  * A good, reliable and affordable camera costs ~$750-$1000, but a oustanding one may even cost a couple thousands.
  * [Picture of a Camera](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,width=500,quality=95/https://www.bhphotovideo.com/images/images500x500/canon_3380c132_eos_rp_mirrorless_digital_1603796831_1558260.jpg)
* Phone
  * A phone has many uses, with one being a camera. If you're inable to afford a camera, you could use your phone as a second option.
  * A phone can also be used to check the weather, the time, the location you're in, and a flashlight. A phone has so many uses that it should be a MUST to bring anywhere, not just for touring.
  * Phones may vary in prices depending on the model, but you don't need the latest model to access all of the features, an old phone works fine as well.
  * [Picture of a Phone](https://cdn.thewirecutter.com/wp-content/media/2026/03/BEST-ANDROID-PHONES-2048px-9716.jpg?width=2048&quality=60&crop=2048:2048&auto=webp)
* Battery
  * Most thng used for touring has a battery lifespan or requires charging. A battery and a backup battery ensures you'll never run out of battery wherever you go, incase you get lost anywhere or one of your hardwares died.
  * Both a phone and camera requires charging to use, making a battery insanely useful. Other hardwares such as flashlights also use batteries allowing you to have multiple uses for a battery.
  * Batteries are relatively cheap, allowing you to stock up on them incase you ever need them and ou won't need to worry much about its cost.
